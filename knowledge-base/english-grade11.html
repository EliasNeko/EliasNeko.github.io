01分词作定语和状语

doing 主动/进行，done 被动/完成。

关键句型
现在分词：主动、进行
过去分词：被动、完成
例句
  • The girl singing on the stage is my sister.
  • The book written by him is popular.
  • Having finished his homework, he went out.
易错点
  • 分词的逻辑主语要和主句主语一致
  • 完成式 having done 表示先发生
小测（3 题）
  1. The boy ___ (play) football is my brother.
  2. ___ (see) from the hill, the village looks beautiful.
  3. ___ (finish) the work, she went home.
点开看答案
  1. playing
  2. Seen
  3. Having finished

02虚拟语气

与事实相反，时态往后退一步。

关键句型
与现在相反：If + 过去式, would + 原形
与过去相反：If + had done, would have done
与将来相反：If + were to do / should do
例句
  • If I were you, I would take the chance.
  • If he had studied harder, he would have passed.
  • If it should rain tomorrow, we would stay at home.
易错点
  • be 动词用 were
  • 与过去相反时，主句用 would have done
小测（3 题）
  1. If I ___ (know) the answer, I would tell you.
  2. If she had left earlier, she ___ (catch) the bus.
  3. I wish I ___ (be) taller.
点开看答案
  1. knew
  2. would have caught
  3. were

03倒装句

为了强调，把助动词提到主语前。

关键句型
Only + 状语 → 部分倒装
Never/Hardly/Not until → 部分倒装
Here/There + 动词 + 主语（完全倒装）
例句
  • Only in this way can we learn English well.
  • Never have I seen such a beautiful village.
  • Not until he left did I know the truth.
易错点
  • Only 修饰主语时不倒装
  • 倒装后助动词要提前，实义动词还原
小测（3 题）
  1. Only then ___ I realize my mistake.
  2. Never ___ I seen such a film.
  3. Here ___ the bus.
点开看答案
  1. did
  2. have
  3. comes

04强调句

It is/was ... that ...

关键句型
It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分
例句
  • It was in Tongdao that I met him.
  • It is my mother who cooks for us every day.
  • It was not until 2010 that the bridge was built.
易错点
  • 强调人可用 who，其他用 that
  • 强调句去掉 It is ... that 后句子仍完整
小测（3 题）
  1. It was yesterday ___ he came.
  2. It is Tom ___ helped me.
  3. It was not until 8 p.m. ___ he arrived.
点开看答案
  1. that
  2. who / that
  3. that

05名词性从句进阶

whatever / whoever / whichever。

关键句型
whatever = anything that；whoever = anyone who
例句
  • Whatever you do, do it well.
  • Whoever comes will be welcome.
  • You can take whichever you like.
易错点
  • whatever 不能换成 what（what 不能引导让步）
  • whoever 作主语时谓语用单数
小测（3 题）
  1. ___ you say, I won't believe you.
  2. ___ breaks the rule will be punished.
  3. Take ___ fits you best.
点开看答案
  1. Whatever
  2. Whoever
  3. whichever

06写作：连接与高级句式

作文要提分，靠的是「连接 + 变化」。

关键句型
Firstly / What's more / As a result / In conclusion
非谓语 + 定语从句 + 强调句混用
例句
  • Tongdao, which is located in Hunan, is famous for its Dong culture.
  • Having enjoyed the beautiful scenery, we decided to stay one more day.
  • It is this trip that made me love my hometown more.
易错点
  • 连接词别堆砌，一段一个就够
  • 句式变化要服务内容，别硬套
小测（3 题）
  1. 用 which 引导定语从句介绍你的家乡。
  2. 用 Having done 结构写一句「吃完饭后我们去了公园」。
  3. 用强调句强调「在通道」。
点开看答案
  1. My hometown, which lies in Hunan, is famous for Dong culture.
  2. Having had dinner, we went to the park.
  3. It was in Tongdao that we met.
通道 · 本地课堂
高二写作可以写「家乡的变化」：Tongdao has changed a lot in recent years. The roads that once were narrow have become wide. 用定语从句和分词，把变化写具体。
