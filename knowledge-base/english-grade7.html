01be 动词与自我介绍

第一句英语：我是谁，我从哪来。

关键句型
I am / You are / He is / She is / It is / We are / They are
例句
  • I am from Tongdao, a small county in Hunan.
  • She is a student at No.1 Middle School.
  • They are not from Huaihua.
易错点
  • am/is/are 不能省，也不能混（I 只配 am）
  • 否定句在 be 后加 not，疑问句把 be 提前
小测（3 题）
  1. I ___ a student.
  2. ___ she from Tongdao?
  3. They ___ (not) my classmates.
点开看答案
  1. am
  2. Is
  3. are not / aren't

02名词复数与代词

一个变多个，this 变 these。

关键句型
book → books；box → boxes；city → cities；man → men；child → children
例句
  • These are my books.
  • Those children are from the Dong village.
  • There are two tomatoes on the table.
易错点
  • 以 s/x/ch/sh 结尾加 es
  • 不规则复数要背：man/men、child/children
小测（3 题）
  1. Write the plural: box → ___
  2. This is my pen. → These ___ my pens.
  3. There are three ___ (child) in the room.
点开看答案
  1. boxes
  2. are
  3. children

03一般现在时

说习惯、说事实，第三人称单数别忘了 -s。

关键句型
主语 + 动词原形；第三人称单数 + s/es
例句
  • I get up at six every day.
  • She likes Dong cloth very much.
  • He does not eat breakfast at home.
易错点
  • 第三人称单数漏加 s
  • 否定和疑问要用 does，后面动词还原
小测（3 题）
  1. He ___ (like) English.
  2. ___ she live in Tongdao?
  3. They ___ (not) watch TV on school nights.
点开看答案
  1. likes
  2. Does
  3. don't / do not

04There be 与介词

某地有某物，in / on / under 说位置。

关键句型
There is + 单数；There are + 复数
例句
  • There is a river near my village.
  • There are many terraces on the hill.
  • The cat is under the table.
易错点
  • There be 就近原则：There is a pen and two books.
  • in 大地方，on 表面，at 小地点
小测（3 题）
  1. There ___ a book on the desk.
  2. There ___ some apples in the basket.
  3. The ball is ___ the box.（在盒子里）
点开看答案
  1. is
  2. are
  3. in

05现在进行时

正在发生的事：be + doing。

关键句型
am/is/are + 动词-ing
例句
  • I am listening to Dong songs now.
  • They are playing basketball on the playground.
  • She is not doing her homework.
易错点
  • 漏掉 be 动词
  • ing 变化：run → running，write → writing
小测（3 题）
  1. Look! He ___ (run).
  2. They ___ (not, sleep) now.
  3. What ___ you ___ (do)?
点开看答案
  1. is running
  2. are not sleeping
  3. are; doing

06一般过去时

昨天的事，动词要变形。

关键句型
规则动词 + ed；不规则动词单独记（go → went, see → saw）
例句
  • I visited Wanfo Mountain last Sunday.
  • We went to the Dong village by bus.
  • She did not come to school yesterday.
易错点
  • did 后面动词用原形
  • 不规则动词别加 ed（goed ✗）
小测（3 题）
  1. I ___ (go) to Tongdao last week.
  2. She ___ (not, see) the film yesterday.
  3. ___ you ___ (visit) your grandma last weekend?
点开看答案
  1. went
  2. didn't see
  3. Did; visit
