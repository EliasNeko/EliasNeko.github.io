01比较级与最高级

谁更大、谁最好。

关键句型
tall → taller → tallest；good → better → best；more beautiful → most beautiful
例句
  • Tongdao is smaller than Huaihua.
  • This is the most beautiful village I have ever seen.
  • My brother is taller than me.
易错点
  • 双写：big → bigger
  • 比较级前不加 the，最高级前要加 the
小测（3 题）
  1. This book is ___ (interesting) than that one.
  2. She is the ___ (tall) girl in our class.
  3. Today is ___ (hot) than yesterday.
点开看答案
  1. more interesting
  2. tallest
  3. hotter

02一般将来时

明天要发生的事。

关键句型
will + 动词原形；be going to + 动词原形
例句
  • I will visit my grandparents this weekend.
  • We are going to have a school trip to Wanfo Mountain.
  • It is going to rain.
易错点
  • will 后动词不加 s/ed
  • be going to 的 be 要跟主语变
小测（3 题）
  1. They ___ (visit) Beijing next month.
  2. Look at the clouds! It ___ (rain).
  3. I ___ (not, go) there tomorrow.
点开看答案
  1. will visit
  2. is going to rain
  3. won't go

03过去进行时

过去某一刻正在做的事。

关键句型
was/were + 动词-ing
例句
  • I was doing my homework at eight last night.
  • They were singing Dong songs when I arrived.
  • She was not sleeping at that time.
易错点
  • when 从句常用一般过去时，主句用过去进行时
  • was/were 要跟主语一致
小测（3 题）
  1. At 9 p.m. yesterday, I ___ (watch) TV.
  2. They ___ (play) football when it began to rain.
  3. ___ she ___ (read) at that time?
点开看答案
  1. was watching
  2. were playing
  3. Was; reading

04现在完成时

过去的动作，和现在有关。

关键句型
have/has + 过去分词；for + 一段时间，since + 时间点
例句
  • I have lived in Tongdao for ten years.
  • She has just finished her homework.
  • Have you ever been to the Dong village?
易错点
  • already/yet/ever/never 常配完成时
  • 短暂动词不能和 for 连用（have bought ✗）
小测（3 题）
  1. I ___ (live) here since 2015.
  2. He ___ (already, finish) the work.
  3. ___ you ever ___ (be) to Beijing?
点开看答案
  1. have lived
  2. has already finished
  3. Have; been

05宾语从句

把一句话当成另一句话的宾语。

关键句型
主句 + that/if/whether/特殊疑问词 + 陈述语序
例句
  • I think that Tongdao is a beautiful place.
  • She asked if I could help her.
  • Do you know where he lives?
易错点
  • 从句必须用陈述语序（where he lives，不是 where does he live）
  • 主句过去时，从句时态要相应变化
小测（3 题）
  1. I don't know ___ he will come.（是否）
  2. Can you tell me where ___?（他住在哪）
  3. She said that she ___ (be) busy.
点开看答案
  1. if / whether
  2. he lives
  3. was

06被动语态初步

强调「被做」，不强调谁做。

关键句型
be + 过去分词（+ by 动作执行者）
例句
  • Dong cloth is made by hand.
  • The bridge was built in 2010.
  • English is spoken in many countries.
易错点
  • be 要随时态变
  • 不及物动词没有被动语态
小测（3 题）
  1. This song ___ (sing) by many people.
  2. The house ___ (build) last year.
  3. Rice ___ (grow) in the south of China.
点开看答案
  1. is sung
  2. was built
  3. is grown
通道 · 本地课堂
把家乡写进比较级：Tongdao is quieter than Changsha, but the air is fresher. 再用被动语态介绍侗锦：Dong cloth is made by hand. 这样语法就有了内容。
