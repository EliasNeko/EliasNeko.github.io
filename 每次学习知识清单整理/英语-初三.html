01定语从句

用一个句子修饰一个名词。

关键句型
先行词 + who/which/that + 从句
例句
  • The man who lives next door is a teacher.
  • This is the village that I visited last year.
  • I like the book which you lent me.
易错点
  • 先行词是人用 who/that，物用 which/that
  • 从句里不要再重复宾语
小测（3 题）
  1. The girl ___ is singing is my sister.（who/which）
  2. This is the pen ___ I bought yesterday.
  3. I know a boy ___ father is a doctor.
点开看答案
  1. who
  2. that / which
  3. whose

02被动语态进阶

各种时态的被动。

关键句型
一般现在：am/is/are + done；一般过去：was/were + done；含情态：情态动词 + be + done
例句
  • The classroom is cleaned every day.
  • The trees were planted last spring.
  • The work must be finished today.
易错点
  • 情态动词后要用 be + done
  • by 短语可省略，不必硬加
小测（3 题）
  1. The room ___ (clean) every morning.
  2. The letter ___ (send) yesterday.
  3. It must ___ (do) now.
点开看答案
  1. is cleaned
  2. was sent
  3. be done

03宾语从句的语序与连接词

从句一律陈述语序。

关键句型
I don't know + what/where/when/if/whether + 主语 + 谓语
例句
  • I don't know where he is.
  • Could you tell me when the train leaves?
  • She asked me if I had finished.
易错点
  • 疑问句变从句要去掉助动词
  • if 和 whether 都表示「是否」
小测（3 题）
  1. Do you know where ___?（他在哪）
  2. I wonder ___ it will rain.（是否）
  3. She asked me what ___ (I, do) last night.
点开看答案
  1. he is
  2. if / whether
  3. I did

04感叹句

What 和 How 的区别。

关键句型
What + a/an + 形容词 + 名词 + 主谓！
How + 形容词/副词 + 主谓！
例句
  • What a beautiful village it is!
  • How beautiful the village is!
  • What clean water!
易错点
  • What 修饰名词，How 修饰形容词/副词
  • 不可数名词前不加 a
小测（3 题）
  1. ___ a clever boy he is!
  2. ___ fast he runs!
  3. ___ nice weather!
点开看答案
  1. What
  2. How
  3. What

05反意疑问句

前肯后否，前否后肯。

关键句型
陈述句 + 助动词/be + 主语?
例句
  • You are from Tongdao, aren't you?
  • She doesn't like coffee, does she?
  • Let's go, shall we?
易错点
  • 否定词（never/hardly）算否定
  • Let's 用 shall we，Let us 用 will you
小测（3 题）
  1. He is a student, ___?
  2. They didn't go, ___?
  3. Let's start, ___?
点开看答案
  1. isn't he
  2. did they
  3. shall we

06虚拟语气初步

与事实相反，用过去式。

关键句型
If + 过去式, 主语 + would/could + 动词原形
例句
  • If I were you, I would go.
  • If I had time, I would visit the village.
  • I wish I could fly.
易错点
  • be 动词一律用 were
  • 主句用 would + 原形，不是 will
小测（3 题）
  1. If I ___ (be) you, I would study harder.
  2. If he had money, he ___ (buy) the book.
  3. I wish I ___ (can) speak English well.
点开看答案
  1. were
  2. would buy
  3. could
