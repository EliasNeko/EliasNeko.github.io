01时态综合复习

一般过去 vs 现在完成，是高中第一道坎。

关键句型
一般过去：强调过去的时间点
现在完成：强调对现在的影响（for/since/already/yet）
例句
  • I visited Tongdao last year.（过去时间点）
  • I have visited Tongdao three times.（到现在为止的经历）
  • She has lived here since 2018.
易错点
  • last year 只能配一般过去
  • have gone to（去了未回）vs have been to（去过已回）
小测（3 题）
  1. I ___ (see) the film yesterday.
  2. He ___ (be) to Beijing twice.
  3. They ___ (live) here for five years.
点开看答案
  1. saw
  2. has been
  3. have lived

02定语从句进阶

where / when / why 和「介词 + which」。

关键句型
先行词是地点 → where；时间 → when；原因 → why
介词 + which/whom
例句
  • This is the village where I grew up.
  • I still remember the day when we first met.
  • The reason why he left is unknown.
  • This is the house in which he lives.
易错点
  • where 在从句里作地点状语，不是主语/宾语
  • 介词后只能用 which/whom，不能用 that
小测（3 题）
  1. This is the school ___ I studied.
  2. I'll never forget the day ___ we met.
  3. The book ___ I'm reading is interesting.（介词+which）
点开看答案
  1. where
  2. when
  3. in which

03名词性从句

主语、宾语、表语、同位语，都能是一个句子。

关键句型
that（无意义，不作成分）/ whether / if（是否）/ 特殊疑问词
例句
  • What he said surprised us.
  • I don't know whether he will come.
  • The news that our team won is true.
易错点
  • 主语从句用 that 时，that 不能省
  • whether 可接 or not，if 一般不直接接
小测（3 题）
  1. ___ he said is true.（主语从句）
  2. I wonder ___ it will rain.（是否）
  3. The fact ___ he lied made us angry.
点开看答案
  1. What
  2. whether / if
  3. that

04非谓语：不定式与动名词

一个句子里只能有一个谓语，其余动词要变形。

关键句型
to do（目的/将来）/ doing（主动/进行/习惯）
例句
  • I want to learn English well.
  • Swimming is good for health.
  • He stopped to rest.（停下来去休息）
  • He stopped smoking.（停止抽烟）
易错点
  • stop to do 与 stop doing 意思相反
  • 介词后要用 doing
小测（3 题）
  1. I enjoy ___ (read).
  2. She decided ___ (go) home.
  3. He is good at ___ (play) basketball.
点开看答案
  1. reading
  2. to go
  3. playing

05被动语态进阶

完成时和进行时的被动。

关键句型
have/has been done；is/are being done；will be done
例句
  • The bridge has been rebuilt.
  • A new school is being built in our town.
  • The work will be finished tomorrow.
易错点
  • 完成时被动是 have been done，别漏 been
  • 不及物动词没有被动
小测（3 题）
  1. The house ___ (build) for two years.
  2. A new road ___ (build) now.
  3. The problem ___ (solve) tomorrow.
点开看答案
  1. has been built
  2. is being built
  3. will be solved

06主谓一致

主语和谓语在人称、数上要一致。

关键句型
each/every/no + 名词 → 单数
A with B → 谓语跟 A
例句
  • Each student has a book.
  • The teacher with his students is going to the museum.
  • Neither he nor I am right.
易错点
  • with/together with 不影响主语单复数
  • either...or / neither...nor 就近原则
小测（3 题）
  1. Each of the boys ___ (have) a ball.
  2. The girl with her friends ___ (be) here.
  3. Neither you nor he ___ (be) wrong.
点开看答案
  1. has
  2. is
  3. is
